Privacy Policy

Todak processes only the information it needs to provide and improve the service and takes steps to protect that information.

1. What information do we use?

Making and saving pottery

You can use Todak without an account. Your pottery and progress are saved in your browser and do not sync automatically across devices.

Feedback

We use your message, any attached image, a submission reference, the page path, and the screen size to investigate problems and improve Todak. We store feedback and attachments privately in Supabase. Only the studio operator can view them. We may use a transformed version of your IP address to limit repeated submissions.

Home page views

We count how many times the home page opens each day, using Korea Standard Time. Opening the page sends an HTTPS request to Supabase without analytics cookies or visitor identifiers.

2. How long do we keep it?

  • Pottery and progress. Your browser stores this information. Clearing browser data may remove it.
  • Feedback and attachments. We delete them within three months of receiving them.
  • Submission limits. We delete the transformed IP value on a 48-hour schedule measured from the last submission.
  • Home page views. We keep daily totals as statistics.

We delete information when its retention period ends or when we no longer need it, except when the law requires us to keep it.

4. Home page views and browser storage

We count page views without analytics cookies. Todak uses your browser’s storage to keep your work and settings.
